The ways into your libraries, beyond opening one.

## Home

Home opens on **Continue watching**, then rows such as **Ready from your watchlist**, your favourite Live TV channels, the latest unwatched films and shows, **Top 10 films in your library**, **Because you watched…**, genre rows that change each day, and **Coming soon**.

The rows come from your server, so every app shows the same ones in the same order. Each profile chooses its own in **Settings › Home & browse** on the web: turn a row on or off, and drag it to move it. The same page sets the rows of each film and TV library's **Browse**, a music library's rows and the shelves of an audiobooks library's front page. A profile that hasn't chosen keeps the rows the server had before.

With more than one film or TV library (say **Films** and **Films in 4K**), **Combine libraries of the same kind** gives Home one **Latest unwatched** row for all your film libraries and one for all your TV ones. It's on to start with.

On a new server, Home on the web shows **Finish setting up** with the steps you skipped in the [setup guide](first-setup.md). Each opens the right page in Settings, and **Hide** puts the list away.

## Search

- **Web:** **Search** at the top of every page, or press `/` or ⌘K (Ctrl-K on Windows).
- **iPhone and iPad:** the **Search** tab.
- **Apple TV:** **Search** in the sidebar.

Before you type, search shows your recent searches and what you opened from them, so you can go back with one press. They follow your profile between devices. Remove one, or **Clear** them all.

Search forgives typos, accents and punctuation: "breking bad" finds Breaking Bad, "amelie" finds Amélie and "spiderman" finds Spider-Man. That works for titles, albums, people and genres, and for titles you ask Siri for.

With Seerr connected, the iPhone's Search also lists titles you don't have under **Not in your library**: add one to your [watchlist](watchlist.md) or **Request** it. On the web and Apple TV, search for them in **Discover**.

## Discover

**Discover** is on the web and the Apple TV. With Seerr connected, it has rows of films and shows from beyond your library: **Trending this week**, **Popular films**, **Popular new shows** and **Coming to cinemas**, and **For you, from Trakt** if you've connected [Trakt](trakt.md). Its search box finds any film or show, to add to your watchlist or [request](requests.md).

Below the rows, **Your library by genre** has a tile for each genre in your libraries.

Profiles with an age limit don't see Discover or Coming soon, since Flexarr can't tell how old a title it hasn't got is for.

## Collections

**Collections** is in the web app's sidebar, under **Browse**. It has:

- **Yours:** collections only you see.
- **For everyone:** the household's, made by the main profile.
- **Franchises:** film series from Radarr, once you have two of their films.

Choose **New collection** to make one: either **Titles I choose**, or a smart collection whose titles match rules (a genre, years, a rating, watched or not, and more) and keep themselves up to date. **Suggested** has ideas from what's in your library. Add a title to a collection from its **⋯** menu with **Add to collection…**.

**Show on Home** puts a collection on Home, in every app.

[Trakt](trakt.md) lists you choose in **Settings › Trakt** show here too, just for you. Kids profiles don't have Collections.

## Coming soon

**Coming soon**, in the web app's sidebar, lists what Sonarr and Radarr expect over the next few weeks, day by day, with what's downloading now at the top. Home has a **Coming soon** row too.

## Genres

Film and TV libraries have a **Genres** view: a tile for each genre, with a picture from one of its titles, that opens the library's titles in that genre.

- **Web:** **Genres** beside **Browse** and **All films** (or **All shows**) at the top of the library.
- **iPhone and iPad:** choose **Genres** at the top of the library.
- **Apple TV:** **Genres** in the switch at the top of the library. To have libraries open on another view, choose it in **Settings › Libraries**.

## Library order

Put your libraries in the order you like, and every app follows it. Each profile has its own order.

- **Web:** **Settings › Navigation**. Drag a library, or use the arrows.
- **Apple TV:** **Settings › Sidebar**.
- **iPhone and iPad:** **Settings › Library order**. Drag a library by its handle. Podcasts and Live TV are in the list too.

Under **Browse** on the same web page, turn off what you don't use: **Watchlist**, **Collections**, **Playlists**, **Live TV**, **Discover** and **Coming soon**. They leave the menu in every app that has them, for your profile only, and still open from Home and links.

## Continue Watching

**Continue watching** on Home holds what you're part way through and the next episode of shows you're watching. A poster's menu (press and hold it in the apps) has two ways to tidy it:

- **Mark as unwatched**, on anything you're part way through, forgets your place without marking it watched. It leaves the row and starts from the beginning next time.
- **Remove from Continue Watching**, on a show's next episode, takes the show out of the row until you play another episode.

**Mark as watched** is there too, for something you finished elsewhere.
