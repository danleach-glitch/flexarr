Good questions

Do donors get anything extra?

A warm feeling and our thanks. Every feature is for everyone, and nothing's held back for people who pay.

What if a month gets more than it needs?

It rolls into the next month first. After that it goes on test hardware for apps people are asking for, like an Android TV box.

Where does a dollar actually go?

All of it reaches Flexarr: GitHub Sponsors takes no fee. It goes towards the bill above.

I can't give anything. Is that all right?

Completely. Telling a friend, reporting a bug or sharing an idea helps just as much.
