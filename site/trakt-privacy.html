Trakt Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy covers Flexarr's Trakt integration: what Flexarr sends to Trakt, what it keeps, where that is kept, and how to delete it. Everything else about Flexarr — the apps, Flexarr Server for Mac and the flexarr.io website — is in the Flexarr privacy policy.

Flexarr is yours, and so is the data

Flexarr is a media server you run yourself, on your own hardware, with apps for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Android and the web. There is no Flexarr cloud and no Flexarr account. Everything described here is kept on the Flexarr server you or whoever invited you runs, under that server owner's control. The Flexarr project receives none of it and has no access to it.

Flexarr collects no personal data of its own. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and nothing here is ever sold or shared with anyone but Trakt, at your request.

Connecting Trakt is your choice

Trakt is off until someone connects their own Trakt account to their own profile, in Settings › Trakt. Each profile connects its own account, one account per profile, and kids profiles can't connect at all. Signing in uses Trakt's device code: the screen shows a code to enter at trakt.tv/activate, so no Trakt password is ever typed into Flexarr or seen by it.

What Flexarr sends to Trakt

Once a profile is connected, Flexarr sends that profile's Trakt account only what keeps the two in step:

  • What that profile has watched, and when, so Trakt's history matches.
  • Where they're up to in something they're part-way through, and, while they're watching, that they're watching it now, how far in they are and whether it's paused (Trakt's scrobbling).
  • Titles they add to or remove from their Flexarr watchlist.
  • The Trakt sign-in token Trakt itself issued, with each request, so Trakt knows whose account it is.

Nothing else goes to Trakt: not your library, your file names or paths, your other profiles, your server's address, or anything anyone else on the server watches. Trakt handles what it receives under the Trakt privacy policy.

What Flexarr keeps on your server

For each connected profile, the server keeps:

  • That profile's Trakt sign-in: the access and refresh tokens Trakt issued.
  • Their Trakt username.
  • When it last synced, and what Trakt last changed, so it needn't ask again.
  • Changes waiting to be sent, so nothing is lost while Trakt or the network is down.
  • Trakt plays of titles the server doesn't have yet, so they arrive already marked watched.
  • The Trakt lists chosen to show in Flexarr.

All of it stays on that server, in its database, and is used only for keeping that profile in step with Trakt. The server's backups include it, so a backup should be kept as carefully as the server itself.

Nothing passes through flexarr.io

Your server talks straight to Trakt's API. Signing in, renewing the sign-in, revoking it and every sync go directly from your server to trakt.tv. No part of it passes through flexarr.io or any other service the Flexarr project runs, and the Flexarr project never sees your Trakt account, your tokens or your history.

How long it's kept, and how to delete it

Disconnect in Settings › Trakt tells Trakt to forget Flexarr and deletes everything above from the server: the sign-in, the username, the sync state, the waiting changes, the held history and the Trakt lists. It happens at once. Making a profile a kids profile, or deleting the profile, disconnects it the same way, and deleting the server or its data folder removes everything with it.

Until then it is kept only while the profile stays connected, for as long as the server's owner runs the server. What you have already watched stays as it is in both places: Flexarr keeps its own watch history, and Trakt keeps yours. To delete what is held on Trakt, or to close your Trakt account, use Trakt's own settings.

Children

Kids profiles cannot connect Trakt, and Flexarr collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
