    Audiobooks

    An audiobooks library with chapters, series, authors and narrators, a player with speed, smart rewind, a sleep timer and bookmarks, and books on your phone offline.

    5 min read

    On this page

    1. Adding the library
    2. Finding books
    3. Listening
    4. On your phone
    5. Details from the internet
    6. Readarr, Bookshelf and Chaptarr

    Note Audiobooks arrive in the next release of Flexarr.

    Audiobooks are a library like films, TV and music. Flexarr finds the books in your folders, reads their chapters and details, and plays each book as one, however many files it has. Readarr, Bookshelf or Chaptarr can fetch books for it, but they don't have to: a folder of audiobooks is enough.

    Books are on the web, iPhone, iPad and the Apple TV. Kids profiles don't see them.

    #Adding the library

    In Settings › Libraries, add a library, choose Audiobooks, and pick the folder your books are in.

    Flexarr reads these layouts:

    Author/Title/…                                   a folder of files is one book
Author/Series/1 - Title/…                        series and number from the folders
Author/Series/Vol. 1.5 - 2004 - Title {Narrator}/
Author/Title/CD1/…, CD2/…                        disc folders are parts of one book
Author/Title.m4b                                 single-file books side by side
Author - Title [B0DPXK6Y7B].m4b                  an Audible id in the name
    • Files: m4b, m4a, mp3, aac, flac, ogg, opus and wma. Books protected by Audible (.aax, .aaxc) can't be played: Library health lists them.
    • Order: files play by disc, then track number, then name, with numbers in names counted as numbers.
    • Details: Flexarr reads them from files beside the book first (metadata.opf from Readarr, Bookshelf or Calibre, Audiobookshelf's metadata.json, desc.txt, reader.txt, a cover image), then the files' tags, then the folder names. It never changes your files.
    • Chapters come from the files themselves, a .cue sheet, or one per file. A long book in a single file with none is split into half-hour parts.

    #Finding books

    The library's front page has Continue listening, Next in series, Recently added, your series and authors, and what you've finished. All books lists every book, with filters for what you haven't started, are part way through or have finished, and by narrator, length and language. Series, Authors and Genres list those; pick a genre to see its books.

    Books you're part way through also show on Home, in Continue watching or listening with your films, shows and podcast episodes. To keep them in their own Continue listening row instead, turn off Books and podcasts in Continue watching in Settings › Podcasts and audiobooks.

    A book's page shows its series ("Book 3 of 7"), authors and narrators, how long it is and how long is left at your speed, its chapters, and more in the series, by the author and read by the narrator. A series' page lists its books in order with the next one to hear picked out.

    Home can show Continue listening, Next in series and New audiobooks: the main profile turns them on or off in Settings › Home and Browse on the web.

    #Listening

    • Speed goes from 0.5× to 3.5×. Each book remembers its own; new books start at your default.
    • Skip back and forward by the steps you choose. Headphones, the lock screen and the car use them too.
    • Smart rewind: after a pause, a book starts a little earlier, more the longer you were away.
    • Sleep timer: after some minutes or at the end of the chapter. On a phone, shake it to add time.
    • Bookmarks, with a note if you like, are on the book's page and in the player.
    • Finished: a book counts as finished in its last two minutes or last 2%, whichever comes first, or when you choose Mark finished on its page. Start over goes back to the beginning and keeps the record. Part-way through a book, it asks first.
    • Across devices: your place is saved as you listen. If another device got further, the player offers to jump there.
    • At the end of a book in a series, the player offers the next one.

    In the player, the seek bar covers the chapter playing; switch it to Book to see the whole book. Time left counts at your speed.

    Set your default speed, skip steps and smart rewind in Settings › Podcasts and audiobooks on the web, iPhone, iPad or Android, or Settings › Playback on the Apple TV. They're your profile's, on every device, and podcasts share them. A book you've set a speed for in the player keeps its own.

    #On your phone

    Download a book on iPhone or iPad to listen without a connection. Your place is sent when you're back online. In Settings › Downloads, Download the next book in a series fetches the next one when you finish a book, and Remove books once finished deletes a book when you've heard it. Both are off to start with. See Downloads.

    The Continue listening widget and "Resume listening in Flexarr" with Siri pick up the book you're on. CarPlay will list books too, once Apple approves Flexarr for CarPlay.

    #Details from the internet

    When a book's files leave gaps, Flexarr fills them from Audible (through the community-run Audnexus service), Open Library, and if you add keys, Google Books and Hardcover. The main profile chooses the Audible store under Audiobook details in the web app's Settings › Libraries; Google Books and Hardcover turn on once their key or token is saved there.

    Audible's chapters replace guessed ones when they match the book's length. On the web, the main profile can change a book's chapters: open its page, choose ⋯ › Edit chapters…, rename them or Shift all by a few seconds, then Save. Use Audible's fills them in; Save keeps them. ⋯ › Fix match… finds the right book when Flexarr picked the wrong one.

    #Author photos

    Each author gets a photo and a short biography. Flexarr looks first for an author.jpg or folder.jpg in the author's folder, then asks Readarr, Audible and Open Library, and tries again later for anyone still missing one. A picture in the author's folder wins over anything found online.

    If an author has the wrong photo or biography, open their page in the web app and choose Edit (it asks for the admin PIN): pick another photo, upload your own, or rewrite the biography. Your changes stay put. Turn off Locked to let Flexarr fill in anything that's missing.

    #Readarr, Bookshelf and Chaptarr

    Connect any of them in Settings › Connections as Readarr / Bookshelf / Chaptarr. Bookshelf keeps one kind of book per instance, so connect the one for audiobooks.

    With one connected:

    • New books show up as soon as they're imported.
    • Their details and covers fill what your files don't say.
    • Coming soon lists books on their way from authors you follow.
    • Deleting a book in Flexarr deletes it there too, and it isn't fetched again.
    • You can request books from the web app: see Requests.
