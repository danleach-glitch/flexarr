    Finding things

    Home and its rows, search, Discover, collections, Coming soon, genres, library order and Continue watching.

    5 min read

    The ways into your libraries, beyond opening one.

    #Home

    Home opens on Continue watching, then rows such as Ready from your watchlist, your favourite Live TV channels, the latest unwatched movies and shows, Top 10 movies in your library, Because you watched…, genre rows that change each day, and Coming soon.

    The rows come from your server, so every app shows the same ones in the same order. Each profile chooses its own in Settings › Home & browse on the web: turn a row on or off, and drag it to move it. The same page sets the rows of each movie and TV library's Browse, a music library's rows and the shelves of an audiobooks library's front page. Profiles that were there before keep the rows they had; a new profile starts with a shorter Home (the music rows other than Made for you, New audiobooks, Continue listening kept apart and Highly rated movies start off) that it can add to. Kids profiles always have the standard Home and menu.

    With more than one movie or TV library (say Movies and Movies in 4K), Combine libraries of the same kind gives Home one Latest unwatched row for all your movie libraries and one for all your TV ones. It's on to start with.

    On a new server, Home on the web shows Finish setting up with the steps you skipped in the setup guide. Each opens the right page in Settings, and Hide puts the list away.

    #Search

    • Web: Search at the top of every page, or press / or ⌘K (Ctrl-K on Windows).
    • iPhone and iPad: the Search tab.
    • Apple TV: Search in the sidebar.

    Before you type, search shows your recent searches and what you opened from them, so you can go back with one press. They follow your profile between devices. Remove one, or Clear them all.

    Search forgives typos, accents and punctuation: "breking bad" finds Breaking Bad, "amelie" finds Amélie and "spiderman" finds Spider-Man. That works for titles, albums, people and genres, and for titles you ask Siri for.

    With Seerr connected, the iPhone's Search also lists titles you don't have under Not in your library: add one to your watchlist or Request it. On the web and Apple TV, search for them in Discover.

    #A title's page

    A movie's or show's page has the same buttons on every app, in the same order:

    • Play, or Resume from where you stopped (on a show, the episode you're up to).
    • Start over, once you've started.
    • Shuffle, on a show: its episodes in a random order.
    • Trailer, when the title has one. On the web, iPhone and Android phone it plays on the page. On Apple TV and Android TV it opens in the YouTube app, so the button only shows when YouTube is installed. Kids profiles don't have it.
    • Watched and Watchlist.
    • Download, on the iPhone and Android phone.
    • ⋯ for the rest, such as Search for subtitles…, and on the web Add to collection…, Fix match… and Delete….

    #Discover

    Discover is on the web, Apple TV and Android TV. With Seerr connected, it has rows of movies and shows from beyond your library: Trending this week, Popular movies, Popular new shows and Coming to cinemas, and For you, from Trakt if you've connected Trakt. Its search box finds any movie or show, to add to your watchlist or request.

    Below the rows, Your library by genre has a tile for each genre in your libraries.

    Profiles with an age limit don't see Discover or Coming soon, since Flexarr can't tell how old a title it hasn't got is for.

    #Collections

    Collections is in the web app's sidebar, under Browse. It has:

    • Yours: collections only you see.
    • For everyone: the household's, made by the main profile.
    • Franchises: movie series from Radarr, once you have two of their movies.

    Choose New collection to make one: either Titles I choose, or a smart collection whose titles match rules (a genre, years, a rating, watched or not, and more) and keep themselves up to date. Suggested has ideas from what's in your library. Add a title to a collection from its ⋯ menu with Add to collection….

    Show on Home puts a collection on Home, in every app.

    Trakt lists you choose in Settings › Trakt show here too, just for you. Kids profiles don't have Collections.

    #Coming soon

    Coming soon, in the web app's sidebar, lists what Sonarr and Radarr expect over the next few weeks, day by day, with what's downloading now at the top. Home has a Coming soon row too: in the apps, its See all opens the same list. A title already in your library opens its page.

    #Genres

    Movie and TV libraries have a Genres view: a tile for each genre, with a picture from one of its titles, that opens the library's titles in that genre.

    • Web: Genres beside Browse and All movies (or All shows) at the top of the library.
    • iPhone and iPad: choose Genres at the top of the library.
    • Apple TV: Genres in the switch at the top of the library. To have libraries open on another view, choose it in Settings › Libraries.

    #Library order

    Put your libraries in the order you like, and every app follows it. Each profile has its own order.

    • Web: Settings › Navigation. Drag a library, or use the arrows.
    • Apple TV: Settings › Sidebar.
    • iPhone and iPad: Settings › Library order. Drag a library by its handle. Podcasts and Live TV are in the list too.

    Under Browse on the same web page, turn off what you don't use: Watchlist, Collections, Playlists, Live TV, Discover and Coming soon. They leave the menu in every app that has them, for your profile only, and still open from Home and links. Hiding isn't a restriction: to keep something from a child, use a kids profile or an age limit.

    #Continue watching

    Continue watching on Home holds what you're part way through and the next episode of shows you're watching. A poster's menu (press and hold it in the apps) has two ways to tidy it:

    • Mark as unwatched, on anything you're part way through, forgets your place without marking it watched. It leaves the row and starts from the beginning next time.
    • Remove from Continue watching, on a show's next episode, takes the show out of the row until you play another episode.

    Mark as watched is there too, for something you finished elsewhere.
