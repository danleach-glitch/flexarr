    Getting started

    • What you needWhat Flexarr is, what kind of computer can run it, and whether you need Sonarr, Radarr and Lidarr.
    • Install on a MacBuild Flexarr from source and run it natively on a Mac as a background service, with hardware video conversion through VideoToolbox.
    • Install on a Synology NASRun Flexarr on a Synology NAS as a Container Manager project, with its settings folder, your media and the right permissions.
    • Install on UnraidAdd Flexarr to Unraid as a Docker container by hand, with its appdata folder, your media share and the right user.
    • Install with Docker on Linux or WindowsRun Flexarr's Docker image on a Linux machine, or on Windows through Docker Desktop and WSL 2, with docker run or Docker Compose.
    • First setupOpen your new Flexarr server in a browser, make yourself its owner, add your films, shows and music, and find the API key if you ever need it.
    • Connect your devicesConnect an iPhone, iPad, Apple TV or another browser to your Flexarr server with a short code, and manage the devices signed in to it.
    • Install the Apple TV app with XcodeBuild the Flexarr Apple TV app from source and install it on your own Apple TV with a Mac and Xcode, until it arrives on the App Store.
    • Updating FlexarrHow to update the Flexarr server in Docker or on a Mac, what happens to your data when you do, and how to keep the apps up to date.

    Your *arrs

    • Connect Sonarr, Radarr, Lidarr and SeerrAdd your *arr apps and Seerr to Flexarr for artwork, Coming soon, requests and instant imports, and set up webhooks so new downloads appear straight away.
    • Media pathsWhy Flexarr needs to see your files at the same paths as Sonarr, Radarr and Lidarr, how to line them up in Docker, and how to use path mappings when you can't.
    • RequestsFind films and shows you don't have yet in Discover, and request them through Seerr from the web app or the Apple TV.

    Watching and listening

    • Playback and transcodingHow Flexarr plays a file as it is, repackages it, or converts it, and how to set up hardware acceleration on your server.
    • HDR and Dolby AtmosHow Flexarr gets HDR10, HLG, Dolby Vision, Atmos and lossless surround to your Apple TV, and the Apple TV settings that matter.
    • Subtitles, skipping and Up NextSubtitles of every kind, skipping intros, recaps and credits, the next-episode countdown and scrubbing previews.
    • MusicMusic libraries with Lidarr's artwork and details, a queue, playlists, lyrics, daily mixes, artist radio and offline albums.
    • PodcastsFollow podcasts from Apple's directory or any feed, with Up Next, speeds, a sleep timer, offline episodes on iPhone and iPad, and OPML import.
    • DownloadsKeep films, episodes, albums, playlists and podcasts on your iPhone or iPad to watch and listen with no connection.
    • Live TV and recordingWatch TV channels in Flexarr with a guide, favourites and rewind, and record programmes and series, using Dispatcharr as the source.

    Around the house

    • ProfilesGive everyone in the house their own Continue Watching, history and settings, with an optional PIN, and choose which libraries each profile sees.
    • Watch away from homeReach your Flexarr server from anywhere with Tailscale, with no ports opened on your router, or through an HTTPS reverse proxy.
    • Siri and ShortcutsAsk Siri on your iPhone or iPad to play a film, the next episode of a show or a podcast, here or on your Apple TV, and build your own shortcuts.
    • Notifications and widgetsGet told on your iPhone or iPad when something new is ready, add podcast widgets to your Home Screen, and fill the Apple TV's Top Shelf.
    • Home AssistantAdd Flexarr to Home Assistant so every Apple TV, iPhone and iPad becomes a media player, and dim the lights when a film starts.
    • Use an AI assistantConnect Claude or another AI assistant to Flexarr over MCP, so it can search your library, see what's playing and play things on your TV.

    Looking after it

    • Bring your history over from PlexCopy what each person has watched, and where they stopped, from Plex Media Server into Flexarr, matched by file and without changing Plex.
    • Library healthFind spare copies and the space they use, files that can't be read, standard-definition films and wrong matches, and fix them with Sonarr and Radarr.
    • BackupsHow Flexarr backs itself up every night, where backups are kept, and how to restore one or move your settings to another server.
    • Logs and diagnosticsRead Flexarr's log, turn up the detail for one area, follow a single playback, collect diagnostics from the apps, and choose whether to send error reports.
    • TroubleshootingFixes for the most common Flexarr problems, from an app that can't find your server to playback that won't start, HDR, sound and connecting away from home.
    • Frequently asked questionsShort answers about Flexarr's cost and licence, what you need to run it, privacy, the apps, and how many people can use it.

    Still stuck?

    Look through Troubleshooting, or open an issue on GitHub with the zip from Settings › Server › Logs › Download diagnostics attached.
