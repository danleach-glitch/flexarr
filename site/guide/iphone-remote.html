    Your iPhone as a remote

    Control what's playing on your Apple TV from your iPhone or iPad: pause, scrub, skip the intro, change subtitles and sound, pick another episode, and carry on watching on the phone.

    3 min read

    When an Apple TV in your home is playing something from Flexarr, the iPhone and iPad apps show a Playing on TV bar above the tabs. It shows the TVs playing for the profile chosen on the phone, so each person sees their own: Claire's film on the bedroom TV doesn't show on Dan's phone. There's nothing to set up: the TV and the phone only need to be signed in to the same server, with the TV's Flexarr app open.

    The bar has play/pause. Tap it for the remote. The × hides the bar until that TV plays a different show or film; it doesn't stop anything, so it's safe while someone else is watching.

    #The remote

    • Scrubber: drag to anywhere in the film or episode. Underneath is how far in it is, how long is left and when it ends.
    • Back and forward 10 seconds, and previous and next episode. Previous starts the episode again, or goes to the one before if you're near the start.
    • Skip Intro, Skip Recap and Next Episode appear here whenever the TV shows them.
    • Audio and subtitles, speed and sound (dialogue boost, night mode and speakers): each is lit while it's set to something other than normal.
    • Continue on iPhone stops the film or episode on the TV and carries on from the same moment on your phone.
    • Stop stops it on the TV. For a few seconds you can Undo, which starts it again from where it stopped.
    • Episodes lists the season: tap one to play it on the TV.

    A title from a library hidden from your profile shows as playing but not what it is. You can pause, skip and stop it, but not continue it on your phone, undo a stop, or change its tracks or sound.

    With more than one TV playing, tap the TV's name at the top to switch between them. For the TV's menus and its volume, use the Remote in Control Center, as before.

    If a button doesn't work (the TV has gone to sleep, or its Flexarr app is closed), the phone says why.

    Note A few buttons need the TV's Flexarr app to be up to date, and the phone only shows the ones it can do. If the phone is signed in with the server's key rather than as its own device, the bar can take up to half a minute to catch up with changes made on the TV.

    #On the Lock Screen

    While the remote is following a TV, your phone shows what's playing on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with back 10 seconds, play/pause and forward 10. Tap it to open the remote. It keeps up while the phone is locked, as long as notifications are on for your server.

    To keep the title private, your phone puts the title and picture there itself. Only whether it's paused, how far in it is, how long it is and its speed travel through Flexarr's notification service, because Apple reads these updates directly and they can't be encrypted like notifications.

    Anyone holding your locked phone can pause or skip the TV from it, as with any Lock Screen controls. To turn it off, go to Settings › Flexarr › Live Activities on the phone. Swiping it away hides it for that title.
