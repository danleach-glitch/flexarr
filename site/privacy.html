Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Flexarr is a media server you run yourself, with apps for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and the web. This policy covers those apps and this website.

The apps collect nothing

The Flexarr apps don't collect, sell or share any personal data. They contain no analytics, advertising or tracking, and they never contact the developer.

The apps talk only to the Flexarr server you connect them to. That server belongs to you or to whoever invited you, not to the Flexarr project. What you watch, your progress and your profiles are kept on that server, under its owner's control.

What stays on your device

  • The address of your server and the profile you chose, in the app's settings.
  • Your sign-in key, in the device's Keychain.
  • Anything you download for offline, and what you played while offline until it's sent to your server.

Notifications of new podcast episodes are made on the device itself; there's no push server. Podcast searches and episodes go through your server, so podcast hosts see your server rather than each listener. When you speak to Siri, Apple handles the speech under its own privacy policy.

Deleting the app removes them. The apps ask for local network access only to find and reach your server at home.

This website

flexarr.io sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted by Vercel and loads fonts from Google Fonts, which see your IP address as part of serving the page, as any web host does.

Children

Flexarr collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date. Questions are welcome on GitHub.
