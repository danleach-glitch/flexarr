Why Flexarr

How Flexarr compares.

Flexarr, side by side with Plex, Jellyfin and Emby, and with the apps people use to watch them. Where another one does something better, we say so.

Checked 28 October 2026 against Flexarr 0.52.0

The short version

Flexarr suits you if

  • ✅You run Sonarr, Radarr or Lidarr. Flexarr takes its details and artwork straight from them, so there's no second library to keep in step.
  • ✅You'd rather not pay or sign up. Every feature and every app is free, with no account and no company server in the middle.
  • ✅Your household watches on Apple devices and the web. The Apple TV, iPhone and iPad apps play files as they are, HDR and Dolby Vision included.
  • ✅You want it all in one place. Films, TV, music, podcasts, live TV, requests and downloads come with the server, not as add-ons.

Look elsewhere if

  • ❌You need Android, a smart TV, a games console or a desktop app. Flexarr's Android app is still being built. Plex, Jellyfin and Emby run on far more devices.
  • ❌You want photos, audiobooks or a language other than English. Flexarr has none of these yet.
  • ❌You track what you watch on Trakt or AniList, or want to watch together with friends elsewhere.
  • ❌You want years of track record. Flexarr's first release was on 24 October 2026, and it's mostly one person's work. Plex and Emby date from around 2008, Jellyfin from 2018.
