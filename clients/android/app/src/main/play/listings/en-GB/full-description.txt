Flexarr is your own streaming service: the films, TV, music and podcasts you love, on your phone and tablet, with no ads, no tracking and no subscription.

It connects to Flexarr, the free media server you run at home on a Mac, a NAS or any computer. Everyone in the household gets their own profile, with their own Continue Watching and progress, kept in step with Flexarr on the TV and the web.

FILMS AND TV
• Home picks up where you left off, with the next episode ready and what's new in your libraries.
• Browse each library by title or genre, see the cast, and search everything at once.
• Skip intros and credits, choose audio and subtitles, change speed and line up audio sync.
• Picture in picture keeps a film playing while you use other apps.
• Send what's playing on to another screen in the house.

DOWNLOADS
• Download films, episodes, albums and podcasts to watch and listen on a plane or underground. What you watch offline is sent to your server when you're back.

MUSIC AND PODCASTS
• Your music library, with albums, artists and playlists, playing on with the screen off.
• Podcasts built in: subscribe, get new episodes, chapters and notes, a sleep timer and the speed you like.
• Music and podcasts in the car with Android Auto.

LIVE TV
• Watch live channels and see what's on next, when your server has a TV source connected.

AT HOME AND AWAY
• Finds your server on your home network by itself, and connects from anywhere once remote access is set up.
• Notifications when something new arrives, a Continue Watching widget and app shortcuts.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Flexarr has no account to create and collects nothing about you. What you watch stays on your own server.

Flexarr contains no films, shows or music of its own. It plays the media on your server. Setting one up takes a few minutes: see https://flexarr.io.
